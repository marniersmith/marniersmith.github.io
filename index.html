I am a PhD student in mathematics at the University of Cambridge (DPMMS and Corpus Christi College), supervised by Clément Mouhot in the partial differential equations group.

My research is on the mathematical theory of Landau damping in plasma physics. Landau damping is the tendency of small disturbances in a stable plasma to fade away. I study what happens to this picture when quantum effects and electromagnetic waves are taken into account.
