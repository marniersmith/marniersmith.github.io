Publications & preprints

  1. The uniform-in-time electrostatic limit of the linearised Vlasov–Maxwell system: the Poisson equilibrium
    Preprint, 2026 (arXiv)
    Damping, Klein–Gordon dispersion and scattering uniform in the speed of light, giving the sharp rate 1/c for the comparison with linearised Vlasov–Poisson.
  2. The semiclassical limit from Hartree to Vlasov at positive density: strong uniform-in-time convergence and scattering
    Preprint, 2026 (arXiv)
    Convergence at rate ℏ² on finite time intervals and, for short-range interactions near Penrose-stable equilibria, at a logarithmic rate uniformly in time.
  3. Phase mixing for the Hartree equation and Landau damping in the semiclassical limit
    Journal de l’École polytechnique — Mathématiques, vol. 13, 2026 (journal, arXiv)
    Nonlinear damping and scattering for short-range interactions near Penrose-stable equilibria, uniform in the semiclassical parameter.
